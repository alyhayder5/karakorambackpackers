Fairy Meadows

The Gateway to Nanga Parbat

Why is Fairy Meadows So Special?

Fairy Meadows, locally known as "Joot," is one of Pakistan’s most breathtaking destinations—a lush alpine meadow nestled at 3,300 meters (10,827 ft) at the base of Nanga Parbat (8,126m), the world’s 9th highest peak and famously called the "Killer Mountain."

What Makes Fairy Meadows Unique?

✔ Unmatched Scenery – A carpet of wildflowers, dense pine forests, and jaw-dropping views of Nanga Parbat.
✔ One of the World’s Most Beautiful Campsites – Named by German climbers in the 1930s for its fairy-tale-like beauty.
✔ Gateway to Nanga Parbat Base Camp – A thrilling trek to the foot of one of the most dangerous mountains on Earth.
✔ Stargazing Paradise – With minimal light pollution, the night sky reveals the Milky Way in full glory.
✔ Cultural Richness – Meet the indigenous Raikot people, known for their hospitality.

Why Should You Visit Fairy Meadows?

✅ For Adventure Seekers – A moderate trek with rewarding views.
✅ For Photographers – Sunrise/sunset on Nanga Parbat is a dream shot.
✅ For Nature Lovers – Pristine landscapes, rare wildlife (Himalayan brown bears, snow leopards).
✅ For Solitude & Peace – Far from crowds, a true escape into wilderness.

Overview

Fairy Meadows is a beautiful meadow located at the base of the majestic Nanga Parbat, one of the highest mountains in Pakistan. This 5-day tour package will take you on a trek to the Fairy Meadows, where you can experience the beauty of the Himalayas up close.

You will also get to visit the Nanga Parbat base camp, Raikot Bridge, and the Junction Point of the Three Great Mountain Ranges.

Itinerary

Departure point: Departure from Lahore / Islamabad

Day 1: Islamabad to Chillas
  • Start your journey from Islamabad early in the morning.
  • Drive towards Chilas via the Karakoram Highway, which is approximately a 10-hour drive.
  • Enjoy the scenic beauty along the way and take breaks for refreshments and meals.
  • Arrive in Chilas in the evening and check in at your hotel.
  • Overnight stay in Chilas.
  • If Naran is open, we use Babusar Top Road instead of the Besham Road
Day 2: Chilas to Fairy Meadows
  • After breakfast, depart from Chilas and head towards Raikot Bridge.
  • From there, take a jeep ride to Tattu Village.
  • Begin your trek from Tattu Village to Fairy Meadows, enjoying the scenic route through picturesque landscapes, lush green meadows, and beautiful forests.
  • Arrive at Fairy Meadows in the evening and check in at your campsite or guesthouse.
  • Spend the night in Fairy Meadows.
Day 3: Fairy Meadows Exploration
  • After breakfast, spend the day exploring Fairy Meadows and its surroundings.
  • Take a hike to Nanga Parbat Base Camp (optional and subject to weather conditions).
  • Enjoy the breathtaking views of the towering Nanga Parbat peak.
  • Take in the serene beauty of the meadows and relax in the peaceful atmosphere.
  • Overnight stay in Fairy Meadows.
Day 4: Fairy Meadows to Chilas
  • After breakfast, trek back from Fairy Meadows to Tattu Village.
  • Take a jeep ride from Tattu Village to Raikot Bridge.
  • Drive from Raikot Bridge to Chilas.
  • Arrive in Chilas and check-in at your hotel.
  • Spend the evening relaxing and unwinding.
  • Overnight stay in Chilas.
  • If Naran is open, we will stay in Naran.
Day 5: Chilas to Islamabad / Lahore
  • After breakfast, check out from your hotel in Chilas.
  • Begin your journey back to Islamabad.
  • Enjoy the scenic drive back, taking breaks for meals and refreshments.
  • Arrive in Islamabad in the evening.
  • Arrival in Lahore concludes your tour.
