Why is Gilgit-Baltistan So Special?

Gilgit-Baltistan (GB) is a paradise for adventurers, nature lovers, and culture seekers, home to some of the world’s highest peaks, ancient Silk Road history, and breathtaking landscapes. Known as the "Land of Giants," it hosts:
✔ Five 8,000m+ peaks (K2, Nanga Parbat, Gasherbrum I & II, Broad Peak).
✔ The world’s largest glaciers (Biafo, Baltoro, Hispar).
✔ Stunning valleys (Hunza, Skardu, Shigar, Nagar).
✔ Rich cultural heritage (Balti, Wakhi, and Burusho communities).
✔ Adventure hub for trekking, mountaineering, and jeep safaris.

Why Should You Visit Gilgit-Baltistan?

✅ For Trekkers – Fairy Meadows, K2 Base Camp, Rush Lake.
✅ For Photographers – Turquoise lakes, golden peaks, starry skies.
✅ For History Buffs – Ancient forts, petroglyphs, Silk Road relics.
✅ For Peace Seekers – Remote villages, serene landscapes.

Itinerary

Departure point: Departure from Lahore / Islamabad

Day 1: Journey to Naran via Scenic Stops

Start your journey with an adventurous road trip
heading towards the stunning valleys of northern
Pakistan. Drive on the beauty of the Karakoram
Highway, known as the world’s 8th wonder.
Morning:
8 am – Tour starts
Travel on the scenic Karakoram Highway to Batakundi
(9 hours).
Afternoon: - Scenic drive through through the Indus Valley on the
Karakoram Highway, known as the world’s 8th
wonder. - Stop at Naran, an alpine town in the Kaghan Valley,
for exploration and breathtaking views.
Evening:
Arrive in Batakundi, a town surrounded by rugged
mountains and rivers.
Transportation: Private-hire vehicle
Accommodation: Guesthouse in Batakundi

Day 2: Batakundi – Fairy Meadows

Continue your journey to one of the most stunning
alpine meadows in the world, Fairy Meadows, located
at the base of the mighty Nanga Parbat, the 9th
highest mountain in the world.
Morning: - Travel to Raikot Bridge on the Karakoram Highway
(2 hours). - Transfer to a 4x4 vehicle and continue to Tatu Village
(2 hours). - Explore the picturesque Tatu Village in the Gilgit
Baltistan region.
Afternoon: - Begin the trek from Tatu Village to Fairy Meadows
(3 hours, 6 km, elevation gain: 300m). -Arrive at Fairy Meadows, a lush alpine pasture with
incredible views of Nanga Parbat.
Evening: - Set up tents and enjoy an evening surrounded by
majestic peaks and serene nature.
Transportation: Private-hire vehicle
Accommodation: Camping at Fairy Meadows

Day 3: Fairy Meadows – Biyal Base Camp

Immerse yourself in nature as you hike through the
breathtaking scenery of Fairy Meadows toward Biyal
Camp, closer to Nanga Parbat Base Camp.
Morning: - Begin the hike from Fairy Meadows to Biyal Camp
(2 hours, 3 km, elevation gain: 75m). - Visit local villages and towns along the trekking
route.
Afternoon: - Explore the hiking trail with panoramic views of
Nanga Parbat, glaciers, and forests. - Enjoy leisure activities like cricket with the locals,
making your adventure memorable. Explore local
villages to gain insights into life in this remote region.
Evening: - Overnight camping under the stars at Biyal Camp.
Accommodation: Camping at Biyal Camp.

Day 4: Biyal Camp – Fairy Meadows – Gilgit

Conclude your trekking adventure with a scenic
descent from Fairy Meadows and travel to Gilgit, the
heart of Gilgit-Baltistan.
Morning:
Hike back to Fairy Meadows and the park’s main
entrance (3 hours, 8 km, elevation loss: 300m).
Afternoon:
Travel from Fairy Meadows to Gilgit (3–4 hours),
enjoying the beautiful landscapes along the way.
Evening:
Immerse yourself in local life with a local homestay in
Gilgit, surrounded by mountain views and local
hospitality.
Transportation: Private-hire vehicle
Accommodation: Homestay at Gilgit or Hotel 

Day 5: Gilgit – Karimabad (Hunza Valley)

Journey into the heart of the Hunza Valley, known for
its breathtaking natural beauty, vibrant culture, and
welcoming communities.
Morning: - Visit the Junction Point of Three Mountain Ranges,
where the Karakoram, Himalayas, and Hindu Kush
meet. - Explore the vibrant Gilgit Bazaar, famous for its
woolen traditional attire.
Afternoon: - Travel to Hunza Valley in the Karakoram Range
(3 hours) - Stop at villages of the Brusho and Shinaki people,
renowned for their distinct identities and traditions.
Evening:
Arrive in Karimabad, the capital of Hunza Valley.
Transportation: Private-hire vehicle
Accommodation: Guesthouse in Karimabad

Day 6: Hunza Valley & Passu Mountains

Discover the architectural marvels, jagged peaks, and
serene lakes of the Hunza Valley.
Morning: - Visit the Altit Fort (12th century) and Baltit Fort (14th
century), both UNESCO World Heritage Sites. - Appreciate the unique Himalayan and Tibetan
architectural styles.
Afternoon: - Travel toward the Passu Cones, sharp jagged peaks of
the Hunza Valley. - Stop at Attabad Lake, formed by a massive landslide
in 2010, for a short hike. - Cross the Passu Suspension Bridge, a local landmark
and engineering feat, and visit nearby glaciers.
Evening:
Return to Karimabad for the night.
Transportation: Private-hire vehicle
Accommodation: Guesthouse in Karimabad

Day 7: Hunza Valley – Gilgit

Continue along the Karakoram Highway, exploring
ancient rock carvings and glaciers before arriving in
Gilgit.
Morning: - Travel back toward Gilgit via the Karakoram Highway
(3 hours) - Stop at Ganish Bridge and view the 8th-century
Manthal Buddha Rock Carvings.
Afternoon: - Explore the Rakaposhi Glaciers, one of the world’s
largest non-polar glaciers. - Cross the Danyore Suspension Bridge, offering
stunning views of the surrounding landscapes. - Visit the Kargah Buddha, a 7th-century rock carving,
a testament to Buddhism once rich influence in the
region.
Evening:
Arrive in Gilgit and stay overnight at a local homestay.
Transportation: Private-hire vehicle
Accommodation: Homestay in Gilgit or Hotel

Day 8: Gilgit – Skardu (Karakoram JSR) road

Travel along the stunning Jaglot-Skardu Road, known
for breathtaking mountain views, deep valleys, and
rugged landscapes, to reach the serene and culturally
rich town of Skardu
Morning:
Drive along the scenic Jaglot-Skardu Road (4 hours).
Afternoon: - Visit the Skardu Shangrila Resort, famously known as
"Heaven on Earth," for its serene landscapes. - Explore Upper Kachura Lake, with an optional zipline
or boating experience (additional fee).
Evening: - Discover the town of Skardu, renowned for its
breathtaking landscapes and rich cultural heritage.
Transportation: Private-hire vehicle
Accommodation: Homestay in Skardu

Day 9: Skardu & surroundings

Explore Skardu’s historical and natural wonders,
including ancient forts, crystal-clear lakes, serene
valleys, and golden sand dunes, offering a perfect
blend of adventure, history, and scenic beauty.
Morning:
Travel to Kharpocho Fort (3 hours), a 16th-century fort
with panoramic views of the Indus and Skardu Valleys.
Afternoon: - Visit the Shigar Valley and Blind Lake, a crystal-clear
lake surrounded by mountains. - Experience a desert safari in the Shigar Valley, with
optional ATV and bike rides (additional fee).
Evening: - Return to Skardu for the night.
Transportation: Private-hire vehicle
Accommodation: Homestay in Skardu or Guest House 

Day 10: Return to Islamabad

Conclude your adventure with a scenic journey back to
Islamabad, either by flight or road. Reflect on the
breathtaking landscapes and cultural experiences from
this unforgettable exploration of northern Pakistan.
Morning:
Fly from Skardu to Islamabad (1 hour)
Or
Travel by road to Gilgit (4 hours) and then to
Islamabad by flight (1 hour).
Depends on weather & airline schedules
Afternoon: - Arrive in Islamabad. - If time permits, enjoy a short city tour.
Evening:
Tour ends at 6 PM.
Transportation: Private-hire vehicle, Plane
