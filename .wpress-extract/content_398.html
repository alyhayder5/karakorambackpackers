Hunza Valley

Nestled amidst the towering peaks of the Karakoram, Hunza Valley is a breathtaking jewel of Pakistan, where time slows down and nature paints its most vibrant masterpiece. Imagine crystal-clear turquoise rivers winding through lush apricot orchards, snow-capped mountains like Rakaposhi and Ultar Sar piercing the sky, and ancient forts whispering tales of Silk Road legends. Hunza isn’t just a destination—it’s an enchanting escape into a world of serene beauty, rich culture, and warm hospitality. Trek through the surreal landscapes of Attabad Lake, stroll in the fairy-tale-like Hussaini Suspension Bridge, or witness the golden sunrise over Eagle’s Nest—every moment here feels like a dream. With its legendary longevity, delectable organic cuisine, and the soul-stirring grandeur of the Karakoram Highway, Hunza is more than a valley; it’s a symphony of nature, adventure, and tranquility. Come, let the magic of Hunza awaken your wanderlust! ✨🏔️

Itinerary

Departure point: Departure from Lahore / Islamabad

Day 1: Journey to Gilgit
  • Breakfast at Balakot, followed by a visit to Dassu and Besham.
  • Tea & lunch break at Chilas.
  • Reach Gilgit by 5:00 pm for check-in.
  • Overnight stay in Gilgit.
  • If Naran is open, we will take the scenic Babusar Top road.
Day 2: Gilgit to Hunza
  • Early morning breakfast.
  • Depart for Hunza (2 hours drive).
  • Arrive in Hunza around 1:30 pm.
  • Visit Rakaposhi View Point and explore the Altit & Baltit Forts.
  • Stroll through Karimabad Bazar and enjoy a meal at Cafe-De-Hunza.
  • An overnight stay in the heart of Hunza, Karimabad.
Day 3: Hunza to China Border
  • Drive from Hunza to the China Border (approximately 3.5 hours one way).
  • Visit the beautiful Attabad Lake.
  • Explore the iconic Passu Cones and Hussaini Suspension Bridge.
  • Visit the Khunjerab Pass (China Border).
  • Return to Hunza and overnight stay in Karimabad.
Day 4: Hunza to Besham
  • Early morning wake-up call for the Eagle’s Nest View Point to catch the breathtaking sunrise.
  • Depart for Besham (8-10 hours of travel).
  • Short scenic stops along the way.
  • Overnight stay in Chilas.
  • If Naran is open, we may stay in Naran
Day 5: Return to Islamabad
  • Breakfast at Chilas or Naran.
  • Depart for Islamabad (5-6 hours travel).
  • Enjoy short stops along the way.
  • Drop-off at mentioned  locations in Islamabad.
